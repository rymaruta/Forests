Forests visual targets (lock these; do not invent blindly)

TREE (ref-tree-deciduous.jpg)
- One continuous cloud canopy with scalloped bumpy outline
- NOT stacked spheres / grape blobs / pancake discs
- Thick tapered trunk, gentle root flare, few short branches

COTTAGE (ref-cottage.jpg)
- Cream plaster + dark timber frame readable on front
- Overhanging tiled roof, porch posts, recessed door, flower boxes
- Stone foundation; cozy proportions, not a bare box

TRAVELER (ref-traveler.jpg)
- Chibi: big head, short body, flared teal tunic
- Straw hat soft brim, scarf, arms down at sides
- Face readable (large eyes, blush)

SCENE (ref-scene-mood.jpg)
- Muted moss grass, soft morning light
- Stepping stones grey-brown (not chalk white)
- Pond with stone shore; trees clear of cottage
